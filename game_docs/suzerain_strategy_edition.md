# Suzerain Strategy Edition — Official Game Document & Rulebook

**Category / Genre:** Political Strategy
**Players:** 2–4 players
**Play Time:** 90 minutes

## Overview & Description
A high-stakes geopolitical narrative game where players lead a nation through war, economic reform, and diplomacy.

## Official Rules & Win Conditions
Players draft policy cards each turn. Victory points are scored by maintaining stability, winning war room votes, and securing trade treaties.
